  snapshots, maintained file-format authorities, regenerated 1,100-document

```sh
GMCP_BUILD_RUNTIME_ROOT="$PWD/build/private-runtime-201" \
  PYTHON_BIN=/absolute/path/to/python3.14 scripts/run_local_release_tests.sh
```

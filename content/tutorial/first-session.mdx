---
title: Your first v2 session
---

Use a **disposable copy** of a font for this first session. Open it in Glyphs and save it. Preparation and application both require the same saved, clean baseline.

## Connect and identify the font

Ask your connected client:

> Check Glyphs MCP status, list the open documents and identify my disposable font. Do not change it yet.

The client should use `get_status` and `list_documents`. Check the font path, document ID and dirty state. Do not infer the target from whichever window happens to be in front.

## Prepare a small change

Choose a glyph that exists in the test font. For example, ask:

> Prepare a width increase of 8.125 font units for H only. Show me the report before applying it.

The client calls `start_job` with the selected document ID, `kind="width_delta"`, `glyphs=["H"]` and `delta=8.125`. Preparation changes nothing in the open font. It targets the glyph's layers; omit the glyph list only when you intend a full-font operation.

Keep the returned job ID. Call `get_job` until it reaches `ready`, or a terminal error or cancellation state. Review `changeCount`, `sample`, warnings and any full report provided. Counts refer to changes, not necessarily distinct glyphs.

## Apply and review

Ask the client to apply that job ID. `apply_job` starts a reversible live operation; use `get_job` to follow it to completion. Inspect H in Glyphs, read its width through `read_entities` and try native Undo and Redo on the affected glyph.

The example tool arguments are:

```json
{
  "document_id": "<document ID from list_documents>",
  "kind": "width_delta",
  "delta": 8.125,
  "glyphs": ["H"]
}
```

For `get_job`, `apply_job` and `discard_job`, use `{ "job_id": "<returned job ID>" }`.

## Finish the experiment

To restore the complete job, use `discard_job` and wait for its terminal result.
It checks that affected targets have not been changed independently. To keep
the result when the task authorizes persistence, review it and call
`accept_job`. It verifies the targets, saves the whole document and closes the
rollback window. Application itself does not save or accept changes.

Do not submit the same idea as a new job to recover from a timeout. Retain the original ID and reconcile it with `get_job`; see [Safety and recovery](../concepts/safety-model.mdx).

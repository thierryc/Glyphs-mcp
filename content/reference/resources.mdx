V2 exposes nine tools. It does not carry forward the v1 documentation-search tools or embedded feedback application.

## Job reports

Use `get_job` for progress, a compact change sample and a report path when the workflow produces a full report. Open that local artifact through your client's file tools if supported. Reports record measurements, exclusions and the scope of the result's claims.

Copy evidence you need before discarding or ending a session. Abandoned bulk artifacts are released after the worker stops using them; terminal job records remain for the session. Reports can contain local font paths and should be reviewed before sharing.
